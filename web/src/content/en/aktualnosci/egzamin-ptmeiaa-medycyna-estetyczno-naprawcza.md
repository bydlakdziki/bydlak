On 3 October 2026 I passed the professional skill exam in **Aesthetic and Reparative Medicine (code 028)**, run by the Polish Society of Aesthetic Medicine and Anti-Aging (PTMEiAA).

In Poland, a professional skill is a formal route for confirming a physician's qualifications in a narrow field, here aesthetic medicine. The exam is another step in my education in this area. It builds on my training at Derma Medical in London and many years of clinical practice in dentistry, orthodontics and aesthetic medicine.
