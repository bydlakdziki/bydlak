On 2 September 2026 an interview with me, conducted by Monika Zieleniewska, was published on Medonet.pl.

In the conversation I talk about my career: from dental studies in Wrocław, through moving to the United Kingdom right after my internship, to several years of studying orthodontics in Vienna. I also talk about working as a dentist in the UK, both within the national health system and in my own practice.

The article is based on my personal experience and contains my private opinions. It is in Polish.
