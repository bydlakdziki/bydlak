From 24 to 26 September 2026 I attended **TAMC – The Aesthetic Medicine Congress**, an international aesthetic medicine congress held in Dubrovnik (Hotel Valamar Lacroma).

The congress brings together physicians from many countries and combines lectures and discussions on, among other things, facial anatomy, treatment safety, injection techniques and skin diagnostics. For me it is a chance to compare my own practice with how colleagues from other centres work and teach.

Most importantly, meetings like this let me challenge established routines and return to daily work and teaching with new questions.
