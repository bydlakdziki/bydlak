On 10 October 2026 I attended the Global Slow Medicine Conference in Warsaw, an international event for physicians, owners and managers of aesthetic medicine clinics.

The guiding theme was the mindful development of a practice: quality of care, trust and the patient experience. The programme covered quality management and clinic certification, new models of patient communication and practice development strategies. The event combined talks, discussion panels with aesthetic medicine physicians and an exhibition area with new technologies, including laser devices.

## Why I attend events like this

Aesthetic medicine is changing quickly, both in technology and in the standards of working with patients. For me, professional conferences are a chance to test my own approach, talk to physicians from different centres and see the directions in which the field is developing. I am particularly drawn to an approach in which accurate diagnosis, conversation with the patient and a well-considered plan matter more than haste.
