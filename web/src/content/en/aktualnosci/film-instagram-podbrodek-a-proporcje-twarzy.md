A short video featuring me has been published on the Instytut Dr Grudzień profile, focusing on the appearance of the chin area and lower face.

A so-called double chin is not always solely related to excess fat. The position of the lower jaw, the proportions of the lower face and chin projection may also play a role. An individual assessment of anatomy should therefore come before choosing a procedure based only on the appearance of this area.

This topic brings together my interests in aesthetic medicine and orthodontics: understanding facial contours involves considering the underlying structures and their proportions.

The video is in Polish. It is educational and does not replace an individual consultation.
