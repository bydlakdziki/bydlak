From 2 to 4 October 2026 I attended the XXIV International Congress of Aesthetic Medicine and Anti-Aging, organised in Warsaw by the Polish Society of Aesthetic Medicine and Anti-Aging (PTMEiAA). This year's theme was “The Anatomy of Collaboration”.

The PTMEiAA congress is one of the most important gatherings of aesthetic medicine physicians in Poland. It includes lectures, practical sessions and clinical discussions, as well as an exhibition of industry novelties. This year the emphasis was on collaboration, between physicians and between physicians and cosmetologists, which fits well with my approach to comprehensive patient care.

The congress was also an opportunity to meet industry friends and exchange clinical experience, and for me an important part of continuing professional development.
