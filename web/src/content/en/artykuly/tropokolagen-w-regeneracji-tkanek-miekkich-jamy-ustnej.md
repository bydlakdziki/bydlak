*An educational article for physicians and anyone interested in the topic. It is an English summary of my publication. The results come from a clinical observation and are not a promise of an effect in every patient, nor do they replace an individual physician's decision.*

## What the paper is about

The paper “Tropocollagen in the regeneration of oral soft tissues – the latest reports” appeared in *Reumatologia* 2024;62 (Suppl 2), in the proceedings of the first Multidisciplinary International Conference on Injectable Collagen Therapy at the National Institute of Geriatrics, Rheumatology and Rehabilitation in Warsaw. It describes an observation of gingival tissue condition after tropocollagen injections in patients treated orthodontically with Invisalign aligners.

## Why this topic

Orthodontic treatment can put a strain on the periodontium, especially in people with a thin gingival biotype. Some patients develop small recessions of the gingival papillae and tooth hypersensitivity related to gingival recession. The aim of the observation was to check whether tropocollagen injections might help prevent an adverse effect on the periodontium and help treat periodontal complications caused by orthodontic treatment.

## How the observation was carried out

- **Product:** type I collagen of porcine origin (MD-TISSUE, 2 ml ampoule, Guna Medical Device).
- **Technique:** injection into keratinised gingiva, four times at intervals of 7–14 days.
- **Indications in the observation:** small recession of the gingival papillae, a thin gingival biotype and tooth hypersensitivity related to recession.

## What was observed

- Tooth hypersensitivity on the VAS scale decreased from 7 to 2.
- After four sessions, a clear improvement was noted in gingival recession, the number of gingival papilla defects and gingival thickness.

## Conclusions and limitations

The observation suggests that tropocollagen may increase the safety of oral connective tissues during aligner treatment and reduce tooth hypersensitivity. The injectable form of tropocollagen appears to be a promising material for regenerating gingival soft tissue, its biotype and the gingival papillae.

It should be remembered that this is a clinical observation described in a conference abstract. It is not a controlled study, so the results should not be generalised. Further research is to focus on refining the application technique and on understanding interactions with other factors affecting soft-tissue reconstruction, such as injectable platelet-rich fibrin (I-PRF).
