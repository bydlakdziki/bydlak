*This article is for information only and does not replace a medical consultation. The answers are general; in an individual case the physician decides after examining the patient.*

I treat patients in Wrocław, Poland, and I regularly hear similar questions. Here are the ones that come up most often.

## Are aesthetic medicine treatments safe?

Every treatment carries some risk. Safety depends on patient selection, technique, choice of product and knowledge of anatomy. That is why it always starts with a history and a discussion of possible side effects.

## What is the difference between biostimulation and a filler?

Fillers work mainly by adding volume where they are placed. Biostimulators are meant to prompt the tissues to respond on their own, and their effect develops gradually. I describe products and methods for information purposes on the [Treatments](/en/treatments/) page.

## How long do the effects last?

There is no single answer. It depends on the type of product or method, the area, metabolism and lifestyle. The physician can discuss approximate expectations but cannot guarantee they will be met.

## Does it hurt?

Sensations are individual and depend on the area and the method. The physician will discuss ways of easing discomfort before the treatment.

## Who should not have a treatment?

Contraindications depend on the method. Those most often discussed include pregnancy and breastfeeding, active infections or inflammation at the treatment site, certain chronic conditions and some medications. The full list is established by the physician during the consultation.

## What should I do after a treatment?

The physician gives individual aftercare advice. If worrying symptoms appear, such as increasing pain, worsening swelling, a change in skin colour or visual disturbances, contact your physician immediately or seek medical help.

## Do you use “before and after” photos?

I do not publish such photos. The result depends on many factors, and photos could create unrealistic expectations.
